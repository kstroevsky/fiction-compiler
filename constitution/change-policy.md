# Process Change Policy

A process rule, prompt, schema, or rubric may change only through a proposal in `projects/<slug>/decisions/` containing:

1. Failure observed
2. Exact evidence
3. Root-layer diagnosis
4. Minimal proposed change
5. New regression case
6. Before/after outputs evaluated blindly
7. Known trade-offs
8. Human approval status

Agents may write proposals. They may not approve their own constitutional changes.
