# Quality Contract

"World-class" is not a directly verifiable property. This project therefore optimizes a vector of qualities and preserves trade-offs for human judgment.

## Hard constraints
- Canon and timeline consistency
- Character knowledge consistency
- Causal preconditions
- Project content boundaries
- Required format, length range, and viewpoint rules
- Research claims marked by confidence and source

## Soft objectives
- Character agency and intentionality
- Dramatic conflict and meaningful cost
- Emotional movement rather than static mood
- Controlled information and revelation
- Thematic pressure without thesis exposition
- Specificity, freshness, and retrospective inevitability
- Prose precision, rhythm, and perceptual value
- Genre fulfillment plus non-default variation
- Scene necessity and global pacing

## Non-goals
- Maximizing adjectives, metaphors, twists, trauma, sentiment, or length
- Producing a universally liked story
- Mimicking a living author
- Treating evaluator confidence as truth
