# Decision — ch01-sc03 ending strategy

## Context
The climax was independently audited by three specialist critics. A genuine, material disagreement
surfaced about the ending *strategy* (not just prose). Per the operating contract, disagreement is

## The disagreement
- **adversarial-reader (pass, 0.70):** the spoken ambiguity-flag ending is *earned, not a cop-out* —
  Mira frees a man she knows is guilty under the mask of impartiality; the dilemma is relocated, not
  dissolved. Keep it; fix the coda and harden the setup.
- **character-simulator (revise, 0.7):** motivation is over-determined and sound, but the spoken
  ending *diverges from the spec* ("renders the single sense") and risks Mira breaking her defining
  "pane of glass" invisibility / stating the theme. Consider the spec's silent single-sense rendering.
- **style-editor (revise, 0.82):** the filter word and the aphoristic last line are the two most
  model-default moves in the piece.

## Convergent findings (fixed unconditionally in candidate-b)
1. Ends on a reflective coda; the spec asks to end on the act. → cut the coda, end on the record.
2. Last line states the theme / model-favored uplift ("the only thing she had ever truly chosen"). → removed.
3. Filter word "She could feel" (profile-banned). → removed.
4. The vertigo hinged on one soft clause ("taking the clean half out of her reach"). → hardened:
   the testimony had already narrowed the word to the damning sense, so Mira's flag is now an
   unmissably *partisan* act that restores a hand the testimony removed.
5. Two opener-run rhythm findings (deterministic linter). → varied.

## Resolution (author's call, human may override)
Keep the **spoken clarification** ("I cannot make it two for you. You will have to.") as Mira's earned
climax — it reads as an in-role interpreter's clarification that *is* the loaded act, not a truth-vs-law
speech. Fix all convergent defects. Promote **candidate-b**.
