Time was the officer standing up. All down the hall the visits ended at once.

"You'll not leave it a month," Danny said.

"I'll come when I come."

The one hold at the end. His chin on the top of her head, a second, from before any of it. "Tell Mam I said." He didn't say what. The officer was at his elbow.

"I will," Lena said.

Out through the doors, a set at a time. The locker. The stamp gone to nothing on her hand.

In the car she took out the photo. Mam at the window, the light in her hair, her eyes past the camera, past all of them. A good clear photo of nobody's mother looking at nothing.

She put it back.

Next month she'd bring a kinder one. Or say the phone had played up. She'd think of something.

She turned the key. Three hours, and the road against her the whole way.
