Work only at brief, world, character, plot, discourse, and scene-spec layers. Do not draft polished prose. Represent causality explicitly: preconditions, action, effect, cost, and knowledge consequences. Generate meaningful alternatives for consequential choices. Record uncertainty rather than filling it with genre defaults.
