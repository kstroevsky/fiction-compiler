---
name: bootstrap-story
description: Creates or completes a fiction project's reader contract and initial planning artifacts through an adversarial interview. Use at the beginning of a project or when the premise remains vague.
---
1. Locate the target under `projects/` or create it with `python3 scripts/new_project.py <slug>`.
2. Read `constitution/quality-contract.md`, `docs/domain-map.md`, and the project's existing brief.
3. Interview the user about unresolved material choices: audience, form, genre promise, desired affect, thematic question, viewpoint, ending constraints, content boundaries, research burden, and originality policy.
4. Challenge contradictions and generic answers. Do not draft story prose.
5. Write `brief/project.json` and `brief/creative-brief.md`.
6. Produce an ambiguity report. Stop if any ambiguity can materially change the story engine.
