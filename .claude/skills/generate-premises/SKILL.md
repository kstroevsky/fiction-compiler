---
name: generate-premises
description: Generates and compares genuinely distinct story premises using structured mutation operators and anti-default checks.
---
1. Read the project brief and relevant genre notes.
2. Generate at least 12 premises across different causal engines, desires, costs, social structures, narrator access, and moral dilemmas.
3. Reject cosmetic paraphrases.
4. For each premise record: protagonist pressure, engine, antagonist force, cost, irreversible choice, thematic tension, genre promise, likely cliché, and a de-cliché mutation.
5. Ask an adversarial reader to predict the likely plot of each premise. Mutate high-consensus premises unless predictability is the intended contract.
6. Keep a Pareto shortlist; do not select by average score alone.
