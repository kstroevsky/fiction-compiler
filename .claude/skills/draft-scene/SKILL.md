---
name: draft-scene
description: Drafts multiple strategically distinct prose candidates from an accepted scene specification and compiled context.
---
1. Confirm the scene spec is valid and structural choices are accepted.
2. Compile scene context.
3. Declare 2–4 candidate strategies that differ in dramatic tactic, focal distance, information pattern, or interaction—not merely wording.
4. Draft each candidate separately under the scene's `candidates/` directory.
5. Do not update canon or manuscript.
6. Record any newly discovered structural problem instead of silently improvising around it.
