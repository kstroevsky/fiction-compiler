# Practically Exhaustive Fiction Domain Map

## 1. Authorial and reader contract
Medium, length, audience, accessibility, genre contract, tonal boundaries, content boundaries, desired affect, thematic question, market/intertextual position, originality policy.

## 2. Storyworld semantics
Ontology, physical rules, magic/technology, geography, ecology, history, chronology, institutions, economy, law, politics, religion, customs, language, naming, material culture, resources, logistics, epistemic limits, invariants, affordances.

## 3. Character system
Embodiment, background, desire, need, values, fear, shame, contradiction, belief model, misbelief, knowledge, secrets, agency, strategy, competence, vulnerability, arc, resistance to change, voice, idiolect, gesture, relationships, status, attachment, role, symbolic dimension, ensemble balance.

## 4. Plot and event design
Premise engine, causal graph, conflict, stakes, cost, escalation, reversals, turning points, sequence logic, subplots, coincidence management, foreshadowing, setup/payoff, mystery, revelation, suspense, surprise, closure, aftermath, genre obligations and deliberate deviations.

## 5. Narrative discourse
Narrator, person, reliability, focalization, distance, interiority, order, duration, frequency, tense, narrative levels, frame, scene/summary/ellipsis, chaptering, transitions, information withholding, dramatic irony, revelation schedule.

## 6. Scene dramaturgy
Scene purpose, entry/exit, local desire, obstacle, tactics, beats, turn, outcome, emotional delta, status movement, blocking, object use, action/reaction, sequel/reflection, decision, transition, necessity.

## 7. Dialogue and interaction
Objective, tactic, subtext, conflict, status, turn-taking, interruption, silence, implication, idiolect, exposition pressure, speech acts, body language, relationship change.

## 8. Prose realization
Voice, diction, register, syntax, rhythm, cadence, paragraph shape, imagery, metaphor, motif, symbolism, semantic fields, sound, repetition, cohesion, sensory embodiment, concrete/abstract balance, humor, irony, clarity, compression, defamiliarization, cliché control, period/genre authenticity.

## 9. Reader cognition and affect
Curiosity, suspense, surprise, anticipation, empathy, identification, uncertainty, cognitive load, inference, immersion, salience, emotional arc, catharsis, ambiguity, thematic discovery, rereadability.

## 10. Research, representation, and ethics
Factual research, source reliability, cultural/historical context, sensitivity review, harmful stereotype detection, privacy, provenance, copyright, attribution, invented-vs-factual labeling.

## 11. Revision and publishing quality
Developmental edit, structural edit, character pass, continuity pass, scene pass, dialogue pass, line edit, copy edit, proofing, formatting, beta-reader synthesis, acceptance criteria.

## 12. Computational operations
Decomposition, typed schemas, retrieval, context packing, summarization, event sourcing, branching, search, model routing, deterministic validators, probabilistic critics, judge calibration, observability, budgets, reproducibility, permissions, failure recovery, regression testing, human gates, process learning.
