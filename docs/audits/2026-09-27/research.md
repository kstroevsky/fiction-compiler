Research accessed 26–27 September 2026. This is a targeted interdisciplinary review, not a systematic review with exhaustive database screening. Thirty-two references are registered in `kb/source-register.json`; the catalog below distinguishes inspected full-text sections, abstracts, and located publication records. Primary sources support the conclusions. Secondary search summaries were used for discovery, not as evidence.

Coverage included narratology, reading cognition, writing processes, stylistics, human–AI writing, narrative planning, long-form consistency, creative diversity, multi-agent revision, critic calibration, and engineering provenance. Searches followed references and explicitly sought counterevidence to the supplied audit. I did not read entire in-copyright craft books, survey every language/tradition, commission experts, or run these papers' experiments. No credible finite search can establish that it has found everything useful.

## What changes the repository design

**1. Plan effects, then test whether readers experience them.** Tian et al. support investigating discourse-aware generation. Their intervention separates turning-point reasoning from arc-diversity prompting; the reported gains do not measure a universal improvement in “human-level prose.” Use a limited information/reveal plan and optional major turns, then compare against a plain outline. Do not prescribe a setback or affect curve for every lyric, comic or episodic work. [Paper and tables](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.13248v2).

**2. Intentionality is useful, but a cast of rational optimizers is not the target.** IPOCL supplies evidence for audience comprehension of motivated actions in a planning setting. The event-indexing model supports tracking time, space, protagonist, causality and intention in comprehension. Transfer these as inspectable links—what the character believes, wants and risks—while allowing habit, bodily impulse, ambivalence, social constraint and unarticulated motive. Neither study validates a full computational psychology or universally necessary goal-driven plot. [IPOCL](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.3841), [event indexing](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.1995.tb00513.x/).

**3. Positive form matters as much as defect removal.** Foregrounding can involve pattern and parallelism as well as deviation. Its observed association with attention and affect motivates experiments on recurrence, syntax and image development. It does not warrant maximizing rarity, adjective density, processing difficulty or reading time. Add a protected-strengths field and compare original/revised passages for what they make perceptible. [Foregrounding study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304422X94000115).

**4. Showing/telling is not one slider.** The handbook distinguishes multiple competing uses of those labels. The repository currently conflates scene/summary, explicit emotion, distance and narrator visibility. Model duration, access, explicitness and register separately. A summary can be vivid; an emotion label can be exact; a fully dramatized errand can be tedious. [Klauk and Köppe](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/84.html).

**5. Keep revision recursive and reversible.** The writing-process literature provides a reason to allow formulation to reshape plans. It does not prove a particular agent architecture. Test a draft-discovery branch against strict outline-first generation, recording changed upstream assumptions rather than silently modifying canon. [Flower and Hayes](https://doi.org/10.58680/ccc198115885).

**6. Judge reliability is local, not a property of the word “LLM.”** TTCW found a large gap from professional fiction and poor assessor agreement in its small historical sample. Reference-based evaluation later reported 0.75 pairwise accuracy, while Fabula reports useful automatic-rater agreement in its evaluation setting. These results differ in texts, populations, models and tasks; they are not contradictory estimates of one constant. Calibrate the actual critic and rubric on the actual kind of writing, with untouched human labels and uncertainty. [TTCW](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14556), [reference-based evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15784), [Fabula §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14411v1).

**7. Correct feedback is not necessarily useful feedback.** StoryFeedback uses 1,300 corrupted stories and a human-assessed subset of 1,920 feedback items, each with three annotations. Specificity and correctness coexist with missed salient problems; the evaluation itself has imperfect inter-rater agreement. Therefore score detection of the important defect, evidence validity and actual repair benefit separately. A critic can correctly complain about an adverb while missing a broken premise. [Rashkin et al., §§6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16007v1).

**8. Reader population changes the answer.** Ismayilzada et al. compare 60 models and 60 people on five-sentence tasks: expert and non-expert judgments differ. A separate under-review study reports different outcomes for prompting versus author-specific fine-tuning, including favorable preferences for fine-tuned outputs. This is counterevidence to categorical claims of permanent machine inability; it does not establish autonomous novel quality or validate this repository. The latter paper's author-imitation setup is not an implementation recommendation. Use legitimate, task-matched comparison material and distinguish expert craft judgment from target-reader enjoyment. [ICCC study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02316v5), [preference-study v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.13939v4).

**9. Multi-agent decomposition is a candidate intervention, not an explanation by itself.** Agents’ Room and CritiCS support exploring specialist planning/writing and revision. The inspected Agents’ Room dataset averages 1,498 target tokens, and it uses a specific backbone with prompted/fine-tuned variants; “long-form” in that study should not be equated with a novella. Evaluate whether gains come from decomposition, extra calls, training or selection. Blind independent revision, as explored by LLM Review, is worth testing against a shared editorial draft. [Agents’ Room](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02603v1), [CritiCS](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1046.pdf), [LLM Review preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08003).

**10. Long-form systems provide engineering ideas, not a shortcut to literary success.** StoryWriter motivates event-relevant history compression. ConStory-Bench supplies a richer contradiction taxonomy. MAGNET/ATLAS reports consistency benefits using character/world-state representations and model-based evaluation. None establishes that a more complete knowledge graph produces distinctive language or convincing emotional experience. Reuse concepts and benchmark categories; verify extraction recall and end-to-end human benefit locally. [StoryWriter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16445), [ConStory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05890), [MAGNET/ATLAS preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00918).

**11. Output quality and population diversity are separate outcomes.** Doshi and Hauser's AI-idea-assisted writing experiment found gains in individual ratings alongside greater similarity across stories. Diversity-aware post-training likewise treats deviation as a separate objective. Neither proves inference-time archive search will work here. Use independent branches and measure between-output sameness; consider MAP-Elites as an algorithmic analogy only after simpler sampling is measured. [Doshi and Hauser](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290), [diverse post-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17126), [MAP-Elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909).

**12. Unpredictability is not the whole of suspense.** Narrative-surprise work separates several properties rather than validating the repository's product formula. Prefix forecasting can reveal premature resolution or an obvious continuation, but a known ending can still compel through stakes, timing and interpretation. The new 100-Endings preprint is useful as a diagnostic hypothesis; do not optimize its mismatch rate directly into random twists. [Surprise framework](https://aclanthology.org/2025.wnu-1.7/), [100-Endings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09854).

**13. Aesthetic pluralism requires different success criteria.** Fabula's participants describe both valuable planning support and constraints on stylistic/cultural practice. Le Guin's carrier-bag counterposition and accounts of experientiality question the assumption that every narrative's value comes from a hero's escalating conflict. These are reasons to permit different profiles, not to invent a universal taxonomy of cultures. Evaluate a culturally situated work with readers competent in its language and tradition. [Fabula §§6–8](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14411v1), [Le Guin publication page](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/the-carrier-bag-theory-of-fiction), [Experientiality](https://www-archiv.fdm.uni-hamburg.de/lhn/node/102/revisions/261/view.html).

## Craft knowledge worth operationalizing next

These are proposed experiments derived from theory, practice and the code review. None is an adopted universal rule.

| Craft capability | Useful generation instruction | Counterexample / failure mode | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perceptual selection | Choose a few details that reveal the focalizer's attention, expertise or avoidance | Endless occupational nouns and hand gestures become a new template | Readers distinguish whose perception organizes the passage; factual plausibility checked where material |
| Narrative voice | State the narrator's attitude and relation to the reader; vary distance intentionally | Exact sentence-length quotas create artificial cadence | Blind voice discrimination across unrelated topics; profile adherence without flattening |
| Rhythm and sound | Experiment with clause arrangement, stress, repetition and paragraph movement | Surface music that obscures action; mechanically alternating short and long sentences | Read-aloud/reader comparison and context-specific preference, not an automatic cadence score |
| Dialogue pragmatics | Specify what each utterance does: ask, evade, promise, misdirect, concede, perform ritual | All speakers become withholding tacticians; direct speech is wrongly punished | Distinct voices and intelligible conversational consequences |
| Emotional complexity | Let incompatible reasons coexist; select when direct naming earns its place | Every feeling replaced by a clenched hand, cup or silence | Reader accounts of emotional movement; preserve useful explicit interpretation |
| Scene/summary/ellipsis | Allocate textual duration to what merits attention | Treating every consequential event as a full dramatic scene | Whole-story pacing and comprehension, including transitions |
| Motif development | Recur with a changed function, context or valuation | Repetition used as an automatic ending symbol | Readers can describe a meaningful change rather than merely notice recurrence |
| Information control | Separate fact, character inference, reader access and revelation | Withholding needed orientation; explaining a planned twist too early | Prefix-only comprehension/expectation probes and later earnedness judgments |
| Comic and rhetorical form | Permit directness, exaggeration, bathos, patterned escalation and deliberate cliché where relevant | A realism-trained critic edits out the comic mechanism | Form-appropriate readers and clean counterexamples for the critic |
| Experiential/static form | Track movement of attention, association, duration or perception | Calling any uneventful passage intentionally static | Reader engagement and formal success under the declared contract |
| Documentary and material specificity | Research the load-bearing practice, institution or physical constraint | Decorative jargon that cannot survive a factual check | Source-grounded details plus separate fiction/counterfactual labels |
| Productive roughness | Preserve a striking asymmetry or unresolved interpretive pressure | Calling mistakes artistic after the fact | Explicit before/after comparison with strengths and losses recorded |

A craft card should contain a narrowly scoped claim, evidence type, applicable forms, failure conditions, contrasting examples and a proposed diagnostic. Store source edition/section/page where available. A `used_by` string proves a declared consumer, not that retrieval actually influenced a model. Log retrieval and evaluate with/without the card. The existing 18-card KB is small enough that a vector database is not yet the obvious missing ingredient.
