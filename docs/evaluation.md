# Evaluation Protocol

## Score vector
Do not start with a single aggregate score. Record 0–5 ratings plus evidence for:

- hard consistency
- causal soundness
- character intentionality
- conflict and cost
- scene necessity
- emotional movement
- information control
- thematic pressure
- freshness/defaultness
- prose precision
- voice consistency
- pacing
- genre contract

## Triple check

### 1. Symbolic/hard checker
Uses schemas, IDs, chronology, preconditions, knowledge state, and explicit constraints.

### 2. Specialist critic ensemble
Separate critics inspect structure, character, discourse, style, and defaultness. Critics may not rewrite during diagnosis.

### 3. Blind comparative reader
Receives anonymized candidates in randomized order, predicts reader experience, identifies exact evidence, and chooses only by the project rubric.

## Anti-bias controls
- Separate drafter and judge contexts.
- Prefer a different model family for at least one high-stakes judge when available.
- Reverse order.
- Penalize unsupported criticism.
- Compare original and revision blindly.
- Record disagreement and confidence.
- Periodically calibrate judges against human decisions.
